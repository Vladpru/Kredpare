# Amendment 2

This amendment is made between Larkspan Auto Finance and Brackwater Bank and amends the base agreement effective 2026-01-01, as amended by Amendment 1. The parties signed it on 2026-09-10, but it takes effect only on 2026-10-01 and applies only to Batches whose Settlement Date is on or after that day. Until then, the base agreement and Amendment 1 govern every Batch. Every other provision of the base agreement stays in force.

## §1 Purpose

The Buyer has narrowed its credit appetite and the parties agree to change the eligible tiers.

## §3 Eligibility

Section 3 of the base agreement is replaced in full by the following: A loan may be offered only if it is assigned one of the credit tiers A, B or C under the Seller's underwriting policy on its Funding Date. Tier D loans are not eligible. The Seller represents that each offered loan is current and has not been charged off.

## §2 General

This amendment may be signed in counterparts. The governing law of the base agreement applies.
