# Amendment 1

This amendment is made between Larkspan Auto Finance and Brackwater Bank and amends the base agreement effective 2026-01-01. It takes effect on 2026-08-24 and applies to every Batch whose Settlement Date is on or after that day. A Batch with an earlier Settlement Date remains governed by the base agreement, even if it is confirmed or invoiced after 2026-08-24. Every other provision of the base agreement stays in force.

## §1 Purpose

The parties wish to align the day count of accrued interest with the Seller's loan servicing records.

## §4.2 Accrued Interest

Section 4.2 of the base agreement is replaced in full by the following: The Buyer pays the Seller accrued interest on each loan, calculated on the actual number of days elapsed over a year of 365 days (Actual/365). Interest accrues from the Funding Date to the Settlement Date. Accrued interest is the UPB multiplied by the loan's annual interest rate multiplied by the number of elapsed days divided by 365.

## §2 General

This amendment may be signed in counterparts. The governing law of the base agreement applies.
