# Amendment 1

This amendment is made between Larkspan Auto Finance and Tessaline Capital and amends the base agreement effective 2026-01-01. It is numbered first but takes effect on 2026-08-24, later than Amendment 2, which takes effect on 2026-07-01. It applies to every Batch whose Settlement Date is on or after 2026-08-24. In this amendment, section numbers are those of the base agreement as renumbered by Amendment 2: accrued interest is section 4.4, and section 4.2 is the definitions section. Every other provision of the base agreement stays in force.

## Purpose

The parties agree to lower the price for one credit tier.

## §4.1 Pricing Grid

Section 4.1 of the base agreement is replaced in full by the following. The accrued interest provisions of section 4.4 and the rounding provisions of section 4.3 apply to the price below unchanged.

| Tier | Price (% of UPB) |
|---|---|
| A | 102.25% |
| B | 101.50% |
| C | 100.50% |
| D | 99.75% |

## General Provisions

This amendment may be signed in counterparts. The governing law of the base agreement applies.
