# Forward Flow Loan Sale Agreement

This agreement is made between Larkspan Auto Finance (the "Seller") and Tessaline Capital (the "Buyer"). It is a synthetic document used for software testing. In this agreement, "Loan" means a retail auto loan offered by the Seller, "Funding Date" means the date on which the Seller advanced the loan proceeds to the borrower, "Settlement Date" means the date on which the Buyer pays for the loans in a batch, "Batch" means the set of loans offered for one Settlement Date, "UPB" means unpaid principal balance, and "Business Day" means a day on which commercial banks in the United States are open for general business.

## §1 Term and Relationship of the Parties

This agreement starts on its effective date and continues until either party gives sixty days written notice. Nothing in this agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency. The Buyer has no obligation to purchase any loan that the Seller has not offered in a Batch.

## §2 Batches

The Seller identifies each Batch by a batch reference that it states in every file it sends for that Batch. The Buyer uses the same reference in its purchase confirmations.

## §3 Eligibility

A loan may be offered only if it is assigned one of the credit tiers A, B, C or D under the Seller's underwriting policy on its Funding Date. All four tiers are eligible. The Seller represents that each offered loan is current and has not been charged off.

## §4 Purchase Price

### §4.1 Pricing Grid

The price of a loan is its UPB multiplied by the percentage for its credit tier:

| Tier | Price (% of UPB) |
|---|---|
| A | 102.25% |
| B | 101.50% |
| C | 101.00% |
| D | 99.75% |

### §4.2 Accrued Interest

The Buyer pays the Seller accrued interest on each loan, calculated on the actual number of days elapsed over a year of 365 days (Actual/365). Interest accrues from the Funding Date to the Settlement Date (exclusive). Accrued interest is the UPB multiplied by the loan's annual interest rate multiplied by the number of elapsed days divided by 365.

### §4.3 Rounding

The price, the accrued interest and the total are each rounded per loan to the nearest cent, with exactly one half cent rounded up (half-up). Amounts are never rounded at batch level.

## §5 Settlement

### §5.1 Settlement Schedule

Settlement takes place weekly on Friday. The Buyer pays the total of all confirmed loans in the Batch by wire transfer on the Settlement Date.

## §6 Confirmation

### §6.1 Confirmation Deadline

The cut-off date for a Batch is two Business Days before its Settlement Date. The Buyer sends a purchase confirmation for each loan in the Batch within five Business Days after the cut-off date. A loan not confirmed within that period is treated as not purchased until the Buyer confirms it.

## §7 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses the parties exchange in writing. A notice sent by email is received on the next Business Day.

## §8 Governing Law and Disputes

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. The parties first try to resolve a dispute by escalation to senior managers, and only then by the courts of that State.

## §9 General

This agreement is the entire understanding of the parties on its subject. Any change must be in a written amendment signed by both parties. Neither party may assign the agreement without the other's written consent.
