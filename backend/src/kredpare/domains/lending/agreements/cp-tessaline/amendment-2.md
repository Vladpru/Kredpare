# Amendment 2

This amendment is made between Larkspan Auto Finance and Tessaline Capital and amends the base agreement effective 2026-01-01. It is numbered second but takes effect on 2026-07-01, earlier than Amendment 1, which takes effect on 2026-08-24. It restructures section 4 of the base agreement. Every other provision of the base agreement stays in force, and the rounding provisions remain in section 4.3.

## §1 Purpose

The parties agree to add a definitions section to the purchase price provisions and to renumber the accrued interest provisions.

## §4.2 Definitions

For section 4, "Accrual Period" means the period from the Funding Date to the Settlement Date, and "Elapsed Days" means the actual number of calendar days in the Accrual Period, counting the Funding Date and not counting the Settlement Date.

## §4.4 Accrued Interest

Section 4.2 of the base agreement (Accrued Interest) is deleted and restated here as section 4.4, without any change to its rule. Any reference in any document to section 4.2 as accrued interest is a reference to this section 4.4. The Buyer pays the Seller accrued interest on each loan, calculated on the actual number of days elapsed over a year of 365 days (Actual/365). Interest accrues from the Funding Date to the Settlement Date (exclusive). Accrued interest is the UPB multiplied by the loan's annual interest rate multiplied by the Elapsed Days divided by 365.

## §2 General

This amendment may be signed in counterparts. The governing law of the base agreement applies.
